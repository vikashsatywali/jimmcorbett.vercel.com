Common Booking Queries

Here are some quick answers to help you plan your contacting and booking.

How far in advance should I book?

Safari bookings open 45 days in advance for Indian citizens and 90 days for foreign nationals. Since Dhikala and Bijrani zones are highly popular, we recommend booking as soon as the window opens.

What documents are required for booking?

You need a government-approved Photo ID card (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License). You must carry the same physical ID card during the safari.

Are safari fees refundable?

No, safari permit fees are collected directly by the Forest Department and are strictly non-refundable and non-transferable under any circumstances.

What is the difference between Canter and Jeep?

Jeep safaris host up to 6 people and can enter all zones. Canter safaris are 16-seater open buses that operate exclusively in the Dhikala zone. Canter is ideal for single travelers or large groups.
